# CIE-Style Field Immersion Readiness Checklist

Classification: Public

## Fictional company disclaimer

This demo uses a fictional Microsoft Fake Company created for demonstration purposes. Any resemblance to real organizations, people, products, services, or data is coincidental. Do not use customer confidential information in this repository.

Use this checklist for fictional onsite field immersion planning.

## Logistics

- Primary onsite point of contact and mobile number placeholder.
- Building address and room location placeholder.
- Visitor access, badge, and check-in process.
- Parking instructions.
- Wi-Fi access for trainers and participants.
- Accessibility or accommodation needs.

## Room readiness

- Projector or display available.
- HDMI or wireless display support confirmed.
- Power available for participants.
- Backup room contact identified.
- Technical support available during the event.

## Participant readiness

- Final attendee list placeholder.
- Expected participant count.
- Persona mix: business leaders, operations, HR, IT, field supervisors, AI champions.
- Copilot Chat access confirmed.
- Microsoft 365 Copilot licensing confirmed where needed.

## Trainer readiness

- Course path selected: MS-4023, AI-3025, Copilot in Apps, agents, or combined.
- Fictional sample data staged.
- Public-safe prompts reviewed.
- AI champion support plan confirmed.
- Office-hours follow-up owner assigned.
