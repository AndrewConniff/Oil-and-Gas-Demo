# Scout and Cowork Trainer-Prep Prompts

Classification: Public

## Fictional company disclaimer

This demo uses a fictional Microsoft Fake Company created for demonstration purposes. Any resemblance to real organizations, people, products, services, or data is coincidental. Do not use customer confidential information in this repository.

Use these optional prompts to prepare delivery materials and handoffs. Keep all outputs public-safe and fictional.

## Prompt order

### 1. Trainer requirements summary

```text
Summarize the trainer requirements for this fictional oil and gas AI enablement delivery. Include course sequence, audience split, artifacts needed, and open questions.
```

### 2. Trainer handoff checklist

```text
Create a concise handoff checklist for assigned trainers. Include what to review, what to customize, and what to avoid because this is a Public demo package.
```

### 3. Follow-up one-pager

```text
Draft a short "In Case You Missed It" follow-up handout for fictional attendees. Include resources, prompt examples, office-hours guidance, and next steps.
```

### 4. Delivery-team open questions

```text
List the open questions the delivery team should confirm before the next fictional AI enablement session.
```

### 5. Agenda customization

```text
Suggest how to adapt a generic Copilot immersion agenda for fictional oil and gas field operations personas without using real customer data.
```
