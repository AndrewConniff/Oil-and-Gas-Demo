# Microsoft 365 Copilot Prompts

Classification: Public

## Fictional company disclaimer

This demo uses a fictional Microsoft Fake Company created for demonstration purposes. Any resemblance to real organizations, people, products, services, or data is coincidental. Do not use customer confidential information in this repository.

Use these prompts for AI-3025 and Copilot in Apps demos with fictional Fabrikam Field Energy content.

## Core Microsoft 365 Copilot order

### 1. Executive update

```text
Draft a concise executive update from the fictional field operations notes. Include current status, risks, decisions needed, and recommended next steps.
```

### 2. Action items

```text
Turn the fictional operations notes into a task list with owner role, due-date placeholder, and expected outcome.
```

### 3. Stakeholder brief

```text
Create a short stakeholder brief for business leaders explaining what changed this week across field sites and what support is needed.
```

### 4. Cross-site risk comparison

```text
Compare the fictional Bakken, Eagle Ford, and Permian site updates. Identify common risks, unique local issues, and suggested leadership actions.
```
