# Persona Cards

Classification: Public

## Fictional company disclaimer

This demo uses a fictional Microsoft Fake Company created for demonstration purposes. Any resemblance to real organizations, people, products, services, or data is coincidental. Do not use customer confidential information in this repository.

## Business leader

- Needs concise status, risks, and decisions.
- Best demos: executive update, leadership deck, cross-site risk comparison.

## Field operations manager

- Needs clear site updates and next actions.
- Best demos: field summary, action item extraction, follow-up questions.

## HR or learning lead

- Needs enablement communications and adoption support.
- Best demos: office-hours summary, AI champion handoff, follow-up one-pager.

## IT readiness lead

- Needs access, room readiness, and support visibility.
- Best demos: readiness checklist, technical support plan, agent test cases.

## AI champion

- Needs practical prompts and safe-use guidance.
- Best demos: prompt library, office-hours answers, champion support workflow.
